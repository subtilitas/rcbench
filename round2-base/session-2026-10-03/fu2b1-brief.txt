Corrections to the analysis:
1. Round 1 sizing. Research.md:578 and task.json followup.items[0] say round 1 sized source selection and disconnect for 12.23 A (V90). No round 1 R5 return contains 12.23. FU-B3/002-P2-R5.json V90 found is 12.7 A at 6.0 V and 13.1 A at 5.8 V, and its pack-path-current report sizes the source selection, disconnect and connector for 13.1 A. The page and the returns disagree, and the record does not explain it.
2. V90 power. P2 states 283.7 W for the four converters and 302.0 W in total (found_values[11]). P4 recomputes 283.6 W and 301.9 W (010 figures[11]).
3. 5 V rail tolerance. VFB 784 to 816 mV (±2 %) alone gives 4.90 to 5.10 V. The 4.825 to 5.175 V band is the ±3.5 % budget, which adds 0.2 % resistors, 0.3 % ripple, 0.7 % transient and 0.3 % load regulation (typical only). See 010 parts[6] 'tolerance at the IO board loads' and 003 report[12].
4. Source-status drop list. TLV9041 was not dropped under rule 4. It "passes on the jlcparts listing (JLCPCB 28904); not among the three kept; its datasheet not read" (003 functions[12].dropped). Only LT1490A and LT6015 fail rule 4.
5. Buck input line. The 3.3 V buck input range is 3.6 V to 36 V to start, 3.0 V to 36 V after start-up, and 42 V absolute (010 parts[5]); the 40 V figure belongs to the P3 modules. The "27 V" is the TPS55285 bus requirement, not a buck rating.
6. Overvoltage wording. The record says automatic release, no latch (010 parts[1].checks). "Latch off" in decision 6 and in the 'OV latch' open figure should read "stays cut off until the input falls below the release threshold, 18.58 to 19.81 V".
7. Evidence indices. The encoder row cites 010 figures[67], which is the Digi-Key call count; the encoder rows are figures[51] and [65]. The 1,475.35 mA sum is figures[30], not [31].
8. Cell-ADC band. The adjudicator's evidence says the datasheet lists the full-scale range as -VFS to VFS (011 data.evidence). That makes about ±128 mV the datasheet-literal reading, not a caveat. The ±80 mV figure assumes FSR = VFS. Which reading applies is not established, so the band is ±80 to ±128 mV at -40 to 85 °C, with no 0 to 50 °C figure. CUV:Threshold 3080 mV guarantees a 3.0 V lowest trip only on the narrow reading; the wide reading needs 3128 mV. P4's "48.6 to 49.2 mV" and "79.4 to 80.0 mV" do not reproduce at the upper end (VFS 1.225 V gives 49.0 mV and 79.8 mV).
9. Recovery-versus-trip argument. Comparing the highest trip corner with the 3200 mV recovery compares different units. ADC error is common to both thresholds on one unit, so per-unit hysteresis stays about 120 mV. Only the absolute recovery window (3.072 to 3.328 V on the wide reading) varies.
10. Asymmetric scrutiny of carried-over figures. The analysis discards the 75 mV band as an R5460-derived carry-over. V94 (3.2 V recovery, "taken as the class figure") and V96 (at least 89 ms, "shortest delay of the class read") have the same origin (003 found_values[15], [17]) and are treated as requirements.
11. USB-C alone, decision 9 option 1. The 7.5 W display figure is IOBoard.md's; P2 carries 1.58 A on the 5 V rail, 7.9 W. Adding 4.9 W for the 3.3 V rail gives 12.8 W at the rail outputs, about 14.2 W in at 90 %, against 15 W at 5 V and 3 A, before charger loss. The display peak (10 W) plus 4.9 W is 14.9 W before losses. Whether it fits is not established, so this option is not supported as recommended.
12. V97 option 2. 64.8 mAh in 30 days is 2.2 % of 3 Ah, but a disconnected pack sits at the 3.0 V a cell floor, not full. The remaining capacity is not in the record, so the comparison is not established. "Never enters SHUTDOWN" needs a data-flash setting the record does not read. The TRM CHECK_WAKE option excludes voltage-based shutdown (010 figures[61]).
13. Single-IC option, decision 3 option 1. The BQ40Z80 OCD at 61.08 mV on 1 mOhm trips at 55.5 to 66.6 A (008 report[8]). The R5 requirement is "52.1 A to 60 A, no trip below 60 A" (003 functions[4].requirements). The analysis lists both but does not flag the contradiction. P4's pass is at OCD -100 mV (90 to 100 A).
14. Pass-2 ordering, decisions 4 and 5. Research.md:556 requires a P1 follow-up before a P2-P4 follow-up, and Research.md:569 says a "new" function is added by the P1 follow-up. Research.md:618 sends FU-2P56 conflicts to pass 2 after FU-2P56. A MOSFET or clamp function therefore needs a P1 follow-up and a plan change to run before FU-2P56; "pass-2 before FU-2P56" is not the recorded order.
15. 1 mV ADC-pin margin. The 3.30 V node against 3.301 V (010 parts[10]) is stated before divider tolerance. 0.1 % resistors move 3.30 V by about ±6.6 mV, so a positive margin is not shown.
