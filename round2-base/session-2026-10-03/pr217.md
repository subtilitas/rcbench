P5's budgets, combinations and assumptions count a reading time (`read_at`) that starts with an ISO (International Organization for Standardization) 8601 time, alone or after one word that names the source. P4 checks, figure verdicts and rulings already accept that word. The P5 prompt and the P5 schema state the format.

**Effect**
- On FU-2P56's recorded P5 (`research/round2-results` at `49fbae7`, `FU-2P56/002-P5.json`), rows without a reading time fall from 35 to 8. The 8 put two or more words before the time, as `FU-2E read 2026-10-02T10:17:15Z`.
- A P5 time may be of any day. P5 cites readings of earlier runs, so the in-run date rule of P4 checks does not apply.
- A word that names no source (`none`, `Not-read`), or two words, before the time still give no reading time.

**Changes**
- `round1.js`: `p5Time` reads one source word before the time (`sourceTime`, then `isTime`) in the five P5 checks: owed budgets, assumptions, combinations and both unsourced counts. The P5 prompt states the format.
- `schemas.json`: the `read_at` descriptions of P5's combinations, budgets and assumptions state the format.
- `README.md`: the source-word rule names P5's rows; P5 times may be of any day.
- `dryrun.js`: P5 read times with a source word, a colon and an earlier day count; two words, `none`, `Not-read` and an hour without minutes do not. Assumptions take the same rule. The prompt carries the format.
