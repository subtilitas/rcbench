=====FUNCTION Cell monitor ['function', 'requirements', 'shortlist', 'dropped']
REQ cell count | reads packs of 1 to 16 cells in series on the ESC pack's balance lead | V181 (found: 1 cell), F15 (Research.md:676), IOBoard.md:473
REQ stack voltage | operates with 67.2 V between the lead's top and bottom pins | F15
REQ input rating | balance-lead input rated to at least 85 V | V188 (found)
REQ separate input | its own balance-lead input, apart from the 2S pack's lead of F9; both packs are connected during a motor test | IOBoard.md:402; Research.md:316
REQ one connector | 16 cells on one 17-pin connector; each pack's leads arrive through an adapter lead | V192 (owner, 2026-09-28)
REQ no count setting | any count from 1 to 16 cells with no jumper or adapter setting; the board ties unused inputs itself | V193 (owner, 2026-09-28)
REQ pack-side interface | sits on the ESC pack's negative and talks direct 4-wire SPI to a digital isolator rated 85 V DC working or more | V189 (owner, 2026-09-28); V289 (owner, 2026-10-01); IOBoard.md:474
REQ SPI timing | SPI mode 3 at 1 MHz at most on measurement SPI1 | Pins.md:156-158
REQ isolator supply | its 5 V regulator output (VREG) also supplies the barrier's pack side | V289 (owner, 2026-10-01)
REQ tap current | at most 100 µA per tap pin with the IO board powered and 10 µA unpowered, averaged over 1 s; the monitor's supply from top to bottom is not counted | V190 (owner, 2026-09-28)
REQ cell resolution | 1 mV per cell reading | V182 (found)
REQ error under load | ±6 mV per cell over 0 to 50 °C ambient, sample timing included. V186 is not found, so the timing term is not fixed; the part's static error is checked against ±6 mV | V183 (found), V7, V186 (not found)
REQ cell voltage range | 2.8 V to 4.2 V a cell within that error | V184 (found), F15
REQ reading period | every cell of a 16-cell pack read at least every 20 ms | V185 (found)
REQ sample skew | not fixed: V186 not found | V186
REQ current pairing | not fixed: V187 not found | V187
REQ resistance resolution | 0.1 mΩ per cell over a current step of at least 10 A | V194 (found)
REQ mis-plugged lead | survives a lead shifted by one pin (4.2 V between adjacent pins, up to 4.2 V on the lowest pin) and a reversed lead (67.2 V reverse) | V191 (found)
REQ pack presence | tells from the lead whether a pack is plugged in; the cell count comes from SET_PACK_CELLS | V195 (found)
REQ ambient range | rated over 0 to 50 °C ambient | V7 (Research.md:704)
REQ quantities | per-cell voltage; resistance under load from the voltage and the INA228's motor current | IOBoard.md:475; IOBoard.md:74
