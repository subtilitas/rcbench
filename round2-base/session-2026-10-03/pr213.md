- For a drop P3 overturned, a part number with a `#` after 4 characters or more, with a letter and a digit, also matches the number before the `#`: an ordering option. `LTC4020EUHF#TRPBF` therefore handles an overturned drop of `LTC4020EUHF#PBF`. Any option after the `#` matches, automotive flows (`#W`, `#3ZZ`) included. A P3 find (`missed`) matches only its own option, because the find's option may be the one with stock. Other makers' packing suffixes (Texas Instruments' reel letter, Maxim's `+T`) remain other part numbers; the README states this as a current limitation.
