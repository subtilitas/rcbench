### Findings that need a ruling

- **Cell monitor pack side (R9):** with V289 the isolator's pack side runs from VREG, which is off while the ADBMS1818 sleeps (1.8 to 2.2 s after the last command), and the monitor wakes only on CSB or SCK activity. As specified, the board cannot wake a sleeping monitor. Packs under 16 V also need a V+ supply of 16 V or more that no function carries.
- **R10, Q4's reference option:** LM4040AIM3-3.0/NOPB, verified in round 1, fails V290 in round 2. The RP2354B ADC's datasheet states no aperture jitter, no start-to-sample latency and no external trigger. The kept part is unaffected.
- **R9 values:** V186 and V187 (cell sample skew, cell-to-current pairing) are not found in either round; V195 (cell count from the lead or from the setting) is refuted: no code fills the state from SET_PACK_CELLS, whose range is 1 to 14 against 16 cells.
